# AI Forge — Forge prompts into products

AI Forge is a consumer game/learning app that makes everyday people (18–55) AI-fluent by playing. Play, take on challenges, and forge prompts into products.

**Features:** Home · Challenges · Play · Forge talk · Forging guide · Settings · Reminders · In-app feedback · In-app manual · Local backup/restore

**Privacy:** All data stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud.

More info: https://sovereigndevelopers.org — by michaelwoolum
